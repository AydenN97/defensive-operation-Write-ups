TryCYBER Challenge Reports

This repository contains my write-ups and investigation reports from TryCYBER challenges. The challenges are used to develop and document practical cybersecurity skills through hands-on investigations, analysis, and incident response scenarios.

Purpose

The goal of this repository is to:

Document my approach to cybersecurity investigations
Practice writing clear and professional investigation reports
Develop practical SOC and defensive security skills
Record relevant evidence, findings, and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
Apply frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK where applicable
Track my progress through different cybersecurity challenges
